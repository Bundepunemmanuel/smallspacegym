About this site

Small Space Gym is a collection of apartment and rental-friendly workout setups, built for people without a spare room for a home gym. Every setup here is chosen around three limits most home gym content ignores: no drilling, a small footprint, and gear that actually folds away when you're done — not just "compact" in the listing.
